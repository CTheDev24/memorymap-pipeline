Presence does not establish complete silhouettes, strength, or print reliability, and an unmatched form is not proof of absence. Review the actual sliced paths and printed objects.

Inspect at normal viewing distance, then under side lighting:

- Can you distinguish individual representative masses and read inhabited street blocks?
- Are masses unintentionally merged, gaps closed, or genuine open areas filled?
- Are thin details missing, fragile, or damaged by ordinary handling?
- Do streets remain subordinate and the orange route immediately recognizable where present?
- Can you recognize landmark silhouettes without reading their names?
- Does the appearance fit the Trace identity, and does side lighting reveal useful rather than noisy relief?

Record printer/profile, material, actual duration, photos from top and perspective, missing/merged features, and pass/fail judgment for each item. The neighborhood coupon does not assess route prominence where the route is outside its crop. Approve, reject, or request a specific correction for each coupon; do not infer full-map acceptance from these samples.
